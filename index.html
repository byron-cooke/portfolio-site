About Byron

I'm a multidisciplinary graphic designer based in the Washington, DC area with 15+ years of experience creating thoughtful, visually engaging work across digital, print, branding, presentations, and web design. My background spans agencies, nonprofits, advocacy organizations, corporate brands, and government-focused communications, giving me the ability to adapt quickly, solve creative problems efficiently, and design for a wide range of audiences and platforms. Over the years, I've worked on everything from campaign branding and social media systems to event collateral, marketing campaigns, reports, and UI-focused digital experiences.

I enjoy building clean, impactful visuals that balance strong aesthetics with clear communication and strategic thinking. Whether collaborating with marketers, developers, creative directors, or stakeholders, I value being someone who can translate ideas into polished, effective creative solutions. I work across industry-standard creative, web, presentation, and AI-assisted design platforms to streamline workflows while maintaining a strong visual perspective and attention to detail.
